## Water Protectors: Multiplication and Division with Purpose

A Grade 3 lesson kit aligned to the Ontario Mathematics curriculum (2020), expectations B2.1, B2.2, B2.6 and B2.7, with coding (C3) and modelling (C4) in the extensions. Students act as water planners and use their math as evidence to ask leaders for clean water in every community.
