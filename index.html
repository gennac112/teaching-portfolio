Water Protectors: Multiplication and Division with Purpose

A Grade 3 lesson kit aligned to the Ontario Mathematics curriculum (2020): B2.1, B2.2, B2.6 and B2.7, with coding (C3) and modelling (C4) in the extensions. Students act as water planners, using multiplication and division to work out how much drinking water a household and a community need. The lesson opens with inquiry into where fresh water comes from, never treats bottled water as a bad habit, honors Anishinaabe water protectors Josephine Mandamin and Autumn Peltier, and ends with students using their math as evidence to ask leaders for clean water in every community.

Read why I built it
  • Whole group slides and a four-part whole group packet
  • Teacher guide: curriculum alignment, design principles, a placement check, and notes on teaching with care
  • Pre-Phase: skip counting, equal groups and repeated addition with jugs, buckets and barrels
  • Phase 1: multiplication with equal groups, arrays and facts of 2, 5 and 10
  • Phase 2: division as sharing and grouping, and fact families
  • Phase 3: a Problem-Solving Path with half-litres, quarter-litres and two-step problems, leading back to whole group
  • Enrichment: design a rainwater system and present the plan to leaders
  • Logic Lab: the Water Carrier Robot, where a repeat loop is multiplication, plus an answer key for the whole kit
